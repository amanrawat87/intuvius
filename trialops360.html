● Clinical Trial Bottlenecks

The Challenge in Study Planning

Planning a clinical trial is traditionally slow, fragmented, and vulnerable to manual error. Study teams and CROs routinely struggle with disconnected tools:

  • Manual Spreadsheet Budgets: Budgets built manually in fragile, disconnected spreadsheets.
  • Inconsistent Assumptions: Conflicting formulas and baseline numbers across cross-functional teams.
  • Site & Country Guesswork: Time-consuming site selection without unified historical feasibility data.
  • Unvalidated Timelines: Critical milestone projections that are difficult to validate before study launch.
  • Costly Synopsis Delays: Weeks or months lost bridging the gap between a synopsis and an actionable plan.
