What is TrialOps360?

TrialOps360 is a comprehensive system for planning and budgeting clinical trials. Teams enter key assumptions from the study synopsis, such as phase, indication, number of patients, countries, duration and visit schedule. The system then produces a complete planning package that is ready to review and refine.

The Challenge

Planning a clinical trial is slow and complex. Teams often face:

  • Budgets built manually in spreadsheets
  • Inconsistent assumptions across teams
  • Time-consuming site and country selection
  • Timelines that are hard to validate early
  • Delays between a synopsis and an actionable plan
