● Who We Are

About Intuivus

Intuivus is a clinical trial technology company. We are building a connected set of systems that support every stage of a clinical trial, from early planning through to execution.

Clinical trials depend on accurate assumptions, realistic budgets and well-planned operations. Our systems turn study information into clear, usable plans so teams can make better decisions early.

● Mission & Vision

Engineered for Purpose, Driven by Vision

Building next-generation operational infrastructure to eliminate guesswork and replace fragmented spreadsheets across global trials.

Foundation

Our Mission

To make clinical trial planning and execution simpler, faster and more reliable through well-designed technology.

  • Unify protocol assumptions into board-defensible study budgets in days
  • Eliminate spreadsheet variance, manual reconciliation, and budget surprises
  • Empower clinical ops and finance leaders with real-time planning certainty
Horizon

Our Vision

A connected set of systems that supports the entire clinical trial lifecycle, from planning to execution.

  • A unified ecosystem bridging study feasibility, budget modeling, and site execution

  • Seamless interoperability connecting sponsors, CROs, and clinical research sites
