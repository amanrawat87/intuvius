About Intuivus

Who We Are

Intuivus is a clinical trial technology company. We develop systems that help sponsors and CROs plan and run clinical trials more efficiently. Based in Boston, our team brings together clinical trial operations and software engineering experience to simplify study planning.

Our Mission

To make clinical trial planning and execution simpler, faster and more reliable through well-designed technology.

Our Vision

A connected set of systems that supports the entire clinical trial lifecycle, from planning to execution.

Partnership

Our Journey

TrialOps360 is the first system in the Intuivus product family. More systems are planned to support further stages of the clinical trial lifecycle.

Leadership / Team

Our leadership team combines clinical development, trial operations, and software engineering experience dedicated to building practical systems for sponsors and CROs.
